Welcome to my academic website. I am a PhD candidate in Philosophy at the University of Hong Kong, supervised by [Prof. Simon Goldstein](https://simondgoldstein.com/).

Before beginning my PhD, I completed my BA in Philosophy at Peking University and my MLitt in Philosophy at the University of St Andrews.

I work in epistemology and decision theory. A central theme of my research is how agents should manage their beliefs, choices, and inquiries when faced with uncertainty, limited information, and competing considerations. I use formal tools to investigate topics including epistemic attitudes, inquiry, decision-making, information aggregation, and the relationship between epistemic and practical norms, with further interests in the philosophy of AI.

This website provides an overview of my research and academic activities. Here I share my current projects, publications, and other scholarly work. Please feel free to contact me at liuyumin98@gmail.com if you would like to discuss my research or related topics.
